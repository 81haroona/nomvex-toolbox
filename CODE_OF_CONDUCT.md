# Code of conduct

People of all experience levels are welcome to improve the project. Discuss code, evidence, and ideas respectfully. Explain disagreements with reproducible examples. Harassment, targeted abuse, discrimination, threats, unauthorized disclosure of personal or confidential data, and misappropriation of others’ work are unacceptable. Avoid repetitive promotion and contributions intended only to inflate activity counts.

Maintainers may request changes, hide content, or restrict participation when these rules are violated. Report conduct concerns through a private channel explicitly published by a maintainer. If none is available, request a contact channel in an issue without naming affected people or disclosing sensitive details. No guaranteed response time is advertised.
